# General Functional Requirements

### Community Member Management
- **Comunero Registration**: Create, update, and manage profiles for community members.
- **Member Status**: Track the status of members (Active, Retired, etc.) using Enums.
- **Family Links**: Associate spouses and dependents with primary members.

### Asset & Property Tracking
- **Terrain Management**: Register and geolocate community lands.
- **Livestock & Personal Assets**: Track animals, automobiles, and other significant properties.
- **Asset History**: Maintain modification histories for all types of assets.

### Governance & Meetings
- **Assembly Management**: Schedule and manage community meetings.
- **Meeting Minutes**: Generate and store official minutes (Actas) for every assembly.
- **Attendance Tracking**: Record attendance and manage substitutes for voting processes.

### Financials & Transactions
- **Transaction Ledger**: Record resource or financial exchanges within the community.
- **Transaction Audit**: Maintain a full history of all financial transactions.

### Content & System Management
- **User Permissions**: RBAC (Role-Based Access Control) for different administrative levels.
- **Dynamic Content**: Manage pages and banners displayed in the companion mobile app.
- **Audit Logging**: Comprehensive modification history for sensitive entities.
